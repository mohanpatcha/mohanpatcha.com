"I am a passionate and ambitious User Experience professional with exceptional experience in enterprise applications — fourteen years of translating human needs into digital reality."

My practice spans the full UX process: from Quantitative and Qualitative Research, Field Studies and Diary Studies, through Empathy Mapping, Persona Creation and Ideation, to high-fidelity Prototypes, Usability Testing, A/B Testing, and UX Audits.
